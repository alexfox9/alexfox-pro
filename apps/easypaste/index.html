Small, everyday tasks

Keep the useful bits close.

Use an earlier copy

Copied another link before pasting the first one? Find the earlier text in History and copy it again.

Keep a reply ready

Save a greeting or a message you write often in Note. Edit the card when your wording changes.

Gather text for a task

Keep useful snippets in History. Add Tags such as “Project” or “Reply” so Search can find them.

Text, ready to reuse

Keep it. Find it.
Use it again.

Two lists for two kinds of text. A compact window beside your other apps.

History for what you copy

Copied text appears in History. Pin important items to keep them when the list fills up.

Note for what you choose to save

Create and edit text cards. Drag them into the order that makes sense to you.

Find a phrase, or just part of it

Search text and Tags in the list you are viewing. Open a preview to read the full text, then select and copy the part you need.

Choose when to paste

Copy an item and paste with ⌘V. Or turn on Auto paste and double-click to copy and paste into the app you were using.

Getting started

A familiar first step.

Start with Auto paste off. You choose where the text goes.

  1. Copy text in another app.
  2. Open EasyPaste and find it in History.
  3. Click the item to copy it again. Return to your app and press ⌘V.

Fits beside your work

A little window.
Your own setup.

Use Collapse View to shrink the window when you leave it. The toolbar pin keeps the window above other windows. It is separate from pinning a History item.

Choose Theme, Transparency, Show numbering, Show pin buttons, and other preferences in Settings.
